Privacy Policy for CertShelf

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Overview

CertShelf (package name: com.certshelf.app, referred to as "we", "our", or "the app") is developed by [Твоё имя или название разработчика]. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the CertShelf mobile application.

2. Local Data Storage (No Backend Servers)

CertShelf is built to store your data locally on your device. We do not operate our own backend servers to collect, store, or synchronize your personal files, certificates, images, or notes in the cloud.

3. How Core Features Handle Data

  • Document Scanning and Text Recognition (OCR): When you select an image, scan a document, or use text recognition inside CertShelf, processing happens locally on your device using on-device recognition tools (such as Google ML Kit). Your documents and extracted text are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Local Notifications and Reminders: CertShelf allows you to schedule local reminders on your device (for example, certificate expiration alerts). Notifications are generated and triggered locally using standard Android notification permissions.
  • Exporting and Sharing Files: When you export or share a document, certificate, or code from CertShelf, the content is passed to the system file picker or Android share sheet only upon your explicit action. Once shared with another app or external service, the handling of that file is governed by the recipient's own privacy policy.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

If you purchase a subscription or premium access inside CertShelf, payment transactions are processed directly by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policies. We do not receive, process, or store your payment card details.

5. Third-Party Libraries and Platform Services

CertShelf uses standard Android platform APIs and third-party libraries (including Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition and Google Play Billing) to provide app functionality. While we do not collect your personal files on our own servers, underlying platform SDKs (such as Google Play services or ML Kit) may collect basic technical diagnostics, app version data, or installation identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security and Retention

  • Your records and files remain on your device until you delete them or clear the app's data.
  • Device-level protection (such as screen lock and Android storage encryption) depends on your device settings.
  • Because CertShelf does not store copies of your data on cloud servers, we recommend keeping personal backups of important documents.

7. How to Delete Your Data

You can delete individual records directly within the app, remove any files you manually exported, or wipe all local app data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → CertShelf → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling CertShelf from your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if app features or legal requirements change. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
